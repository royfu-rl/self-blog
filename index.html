Roy

I am an algorithm engineer on the Foundation Algorithms team at Kling AI, where I lead post-training with Agentic RL for Kling-VLM. I also led KAT-Coder V2.5 across the whole Agentic RL loop: environment synthesis, training infrastructure, algorithms and evaluation. Its open Flash model has passed 3 million downloads, with close to 100 derivative models.

Previously, I trained AI search agents with RL at Meituan and interned at Tencent, working on game AI with large-scale self-play RL. I placed 2nd in the NeurIPS 2022 Neural MMO PvP track, and received my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China.

I am currently working on Agentic RL for multimodal agents: models that write code to make videos, and critics that judge the result.
